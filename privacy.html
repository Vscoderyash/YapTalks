01

Who we are

YapTalks is an online random video chat and text chat platform made by Yash Raj. This Privacy Policy applies to the YapTalks website, real-time chat features, and account login system.

02

Data we collect

  • Account information: email address, authentication ID, and optional display name.
  • Authentication data: sign-in and account events managed through Firebase Authentication.
  • Usage information: app actions such as matching, chat activity, reports, and feature usage.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device signals, and server logs needed for reliability and abuse prevention.
  • Local storage data: profile stats such as streak, XP, and feature preferences stored in your browser.
03

Video and text chat handling

YapTalks uses WebRTC and Socket-based signaling to connect users in real time. Audio/video streams are intended to be peer-to-peer where technically possible. We do not market this service as a permanent recording platform, but technical metadata and moderation-related events may be processed for security, abuse handling, and system operation.

04

How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To provide matching, messaging, and call connection features.
  • To detect abuse, enforce safety rules, and process reports.
  • To improve performance, reliability, and user experience.
  • To support analytics and fraud prevention.
05

Legal bases

Depending on your location, we process personal data based on consent, performance of a contract (providing the service), legitimate interests (service security and improvement), and legal obligations.

06

Third-party services and sharing

We may share data with service providers that help us operate YapTalks, including Google AdSense for ad serving, ad measurement, and cookie-based advertising where enabled:

  • Google Firebase — authentication and analytics.
  • Render — application hosting and infrastructure logs.
  • Vercel — frontend hosting and edge delivery.
  • Socket / STUN infrastructure — used to deliver real-time chat and signaling.

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect users and platform integrity.

07

Cookies and local storage

YapTalks uses browser storage and similar technologies to keep you logged in, remember preferences, and maintain app state. Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or other identifiers to serve and measure ads after ads are approved for the site. You can clear browser storage in your browser settings, but doing so may reset app features and require login again.

08

Data retention

We retain personal data for as long as needed to run and secure YapTalks, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our policies. Retention periods may vary by data type and legal requirements.

09

Security

We apply technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect personal data. This includes per-socket rate limiting, IP-level connection throttling, suspicion scoring, and input sanitization. No online system can be guaranteed 100 percent secure, so users should also protect their devices, passwords, and account access.

10

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection.

You may also request account deletion and data-related help by contacting us. We may need to verify identity before completing certain requests.

11

Age policy

YapTalks is not intended for children under 18. If we learn that personal data from a child has been collected in violation of this policy, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

12

International transfers

Data may be processed on servers located outside your country. By using YapTalks, you understand that your data may be transferred and processed in jurisdictions with different data protection laws.

13

Policy changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material updates, we will revise the effective date and publish the latest version on this page.
