Safer random chat starts with clear rules

This guide explains how YapTalks handles reports, abusive behavior, privacy risk, and responsible use.

Audience: Adults onlyFeature: Safety GuardUpdated: August 8, 2026
01

Community rules

  • Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, scam, or pressure other users.
  • Do not share illegal, hateful, sexual, violent, or exploitative content.
  • Do not ask strangers for passwords, financial details, addresses, private photos, or identity documents.
  • Do not use automation, spam, fake traffic, or repeated disruptive matching behavior.
  • Leave the chat if a conversation becomes uncomfortable and use Report when needed.
02

Built-in protections

YapTalks includes event rate limiting, IP-level connection throttling, suspicion scoring, report counts, and peer disconnect tools. These controls are designed to reduce spam and repeated abuse while keeping the app fast.

Safety Guard helps users keep awareness of trust and report activity during a match. Reports can trigger warnings, disconnects, and review signals.

03

Privacy reminders

Random chat can feel casual, but strangers are still strangers. Avoid sharing your full name, home address, school, workplace, financial details, passwords, private links, or anything that could identify you offline.

Camera and microphone access should be granted only when you are ready to chat. You can mute, turn off your camera, skip, or close the page at any time.

04

How to report a problem

During a match, press Report to flag inappropriate behavior. For privacy or safety requests that need follow-up, contact the owner at yaptalks.help@gmail.com.
